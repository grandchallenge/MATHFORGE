## Log-rank: exact algebra versus a relaxation

Theorem 1.1 (pp. 1–2) claims deterministic two-party communication complexity O(r^(1/2−epsilon)), epsilon=0.000005, for Boolean matrix real rank r, improving the O(sqrt(r)) baseline attributed to Sudakov–Tomon, not merely Lovett's earlier logarithmic bound. This is not a proof of the full log-rank conjecture.

Section 1.2 (pp. 2–5) exhibits the biased Rademacher inner-product relaxation A(x,y)=mu+sqrt((1−mu²)/r)<x,y>. Independent uniform signs have sub-Gaussian moment generating functions. The entropy variational inequality bounds squared mean shift by twice marginal KL, hence the product shift by (KL_X+KL_Y)/sqrt(r). Thus rank, unit row/column L2 norms, bias and flat centered spectrum do not suffice to beat the square-root scale. The missing condition is pointwise sign Booleanity.

Theorem 3.1 (p. 11) excludes sign matrices of rank at most r+1 at r>=ceil(exp(4000000)) satisfying positive bias in [3r^(-1.6epsilon),1−2r^(-3.2epsilon)], matrix-Bregman regularity, sub-Gaussian marginals, and one-sided KL stability of the doubly centered part with coefficient r^(-1/2+epsilon). Section 3 derives these conditions by box biasing, rounding and variational minimization of distributional trace norm plus bias penalty plus KL. Section 4 Proposition 4.3 removes heavy-tail directions and regularizes bulk modes. Section 5 uses the exact pointwise square identity, third moments, chi-square transfer and matrix Khintchine/Hölder bounds. Proposition 5.3 extracts a weighted hollow quadratic slice map with variance sum < r^(1/2−760epsilon) and expected squared Frobenius norm > r^(-3400epsilon). Proposition 6.3 applied after transposition bounds the same energy <= r^(-3400epsilon), using quadratic Gaussian chaos, even cosh tilt, operator norm control and hypercontractivity. The incompatible inequalities are the structural contradiction; the many intermediate estimates and hypotheses remain unverified. Appendix A.3 transfers rectangle density to protocol depth.

## SCC transfer and separator

Exact SCC predecessor: grandchallenge/MATHSOLVE#1309, syzygy transport at ba8f4e6b587ab1ca4f01f0b63037d77ef09476f9, research/SCC-001/SYZYGY_TRANSPORT.md. Its Boolean function ring is over F2, charged step adjoins one product; correction delta on Ann(q)/(q) retains information lost by the quotient alone. Do not identify this correction with real third moments, or KL cost with gate count.

GCL comparator (elementary proof): for x,y in F2^n, f(x,y)=XOR_i x_i y_i has at most n charged AND gates. The sign matrix S(x,y)=(-1)^f has row inner product sum_y (-1)^((x+z)·y), equal to 2^n for x=z and zero otherwise by pairing y with y+e_j in a differing coordinate. Thus real rank is exactly 2^n. Exact integer orthogonality replay for n=1,...,7 passed (capture 391bb3e6c02244d4a26fce8705010612). This refutes raw real rank as polynomially bounded total circuit capacity, not all possible rank-derived invariants. Communication upper bounds do not imply unrestricted classical circuit lower bounds.

A useful invariance test: duplicate rows and columns via independent finite spectators with product reference laws. The weighted matrix operator is isometrically lifted on the constant-spectator subspaces and vanishes on their orthogonal complements; nonzero singular values stay unchanged. For any tilted joint marginal on original coordinates and spectators, the KL chain rule gives marginal KL plus nonnegative conditional KL; the expectation depends only on original coordinates. The minimum KL for a specified expectation shift is unchanged, since independent reference spectators attain the lower bound. This is a GCL derivation, not a claim from the paper. Compare SCC's exponential spectator amplification of correction rank. Necessary next test: define target-relative quantities invariant under independent spectators, derive a gate-local or amortized decrease bound in the actual successor algebra, and reject cheap inner product, parity, equality and product-of-sums families. No such capacity theorem or SAT demand bound is supplied here.
