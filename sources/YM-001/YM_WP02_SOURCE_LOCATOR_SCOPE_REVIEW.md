## Review rule

For each imported source, this pass independently checks that:

1. the locator identifies the cited source or an authoritative record for it;
2. dimension, gauge group/regime, regulator status, and theorem terrain in the WP02 registry are not materially broader than the source supports;
3. the registry claim boundary blocks the nearest false composition into the four-dimensional continuum existence/mass-gap target;
4. any complete-solution or moving-source claim remains noncomposable unless its theorem body survives independent audit.

A locator review is not theorem certification. Detailed proof checking is required only when a source could materially alter the protected frontier; that is why `YM-SRC-016` through `YM-SRC-019` have a separate theorem-body audit.

## Independent scope findings

### Governing target

`YM-SRC-000` and `YM-SRC-001` remain concordant with the protected target. In particular, the Jaffe–Witten problem statement requires a nontrivial four-dimensional theory, local gauge-invariant quantum fields with the stated axiomatic strength and ultraviolet concordance, and a positive physical Hamiltonian spectral gap. It does not license substitution of a fixed-lattice or lower-dimensional result.

### Exact analogues and fixed-regulator results

`YM-SRC-002` through `YM-SRC-011` and `YM-SRC-020` are correctly bounded by dimension, regulator, coupling regime, model class, or large-`N` status. None is a four-dimensional continuum-completion theorem.

### Ultraviolet and reconstruction interfaces

`YM-SRC-012` through `YM-SRC-015` supply genuine interfaces used by the campaign: perturbative short-distance control and OS reconstruction conditions. They do not construct the nonperturbative limiting hierarchy to which those interfaces would apply.

### Moving / complete-solution claims

`YM-SRC-016` through `YM-SRC-019` are now controlled by the protected Forge theorem-body audit. None supplies a composable theorem that removes `YM-D001` through `YM-D005`.
