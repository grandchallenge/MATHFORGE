## 1. Genuine bar complex, bridge and separation

Equivalently use homogeneous (m+1)-tuples modulo simultaneous left translation, passing between them by successive differences g_i^-1 g_(i+1) and partial products. Two successive deletions cancel in pairs, yielding boundary squared zero; a homomorphism preserves products and deletion, hence acts by chain maps, preserving cycles and boundaries and composition.
