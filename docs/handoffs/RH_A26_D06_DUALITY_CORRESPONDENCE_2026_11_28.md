## Exact finite-to-integral mechanism (E >= 1)
LengthEqLengthInvariantsOfTorsionLevels.lean supplies finite_level, ptCokerAt, ptShaAt and local_tate_duality_h2_limit. It compares finite cardinalities rather than requiring coherent choices of existential finite-level pairings. The local field comparison is bicontinuous and cyclotomic-compatible at every r. exists_iota identifies the finite Cartier dual of T_r with A[l^r], by evaluation at 1 and the canonical embedding j/l^r modulo Z_l. These identifications commute with coefficient inclusion (multiply j by l) and the dual of reduction. Finite-level H1 and H2 are finite locally; globally this follows from finite local images and the two finite Sha kernels. H3 mod l vanishes by the relevant local/global cohomological dimension theorem.

Write X=H2(G_S,T), Y=product H2(G_v,T), f:X->Y. Reduction has kernel l^r X: lift a finite-level primitive continuously by a set section of the discrete finite coefficient ring, subtract its coboundary, divide by l^r in T, and use torsion-freeness to verify the cocycle identity. Surjectivity is not assumed: ModLThreeCocycleCondition corrects a continuous lift z of a mod l^r cocycle. Its differential is divisible by l^r. The resulting 3-cocycle c=dz/l^r reduces to a boundary mod l. Lift a primitive b_0, replace c by (c-db_0)/l, repeat. The uniformly convergent sum of l^j b_j has derivative c, and subtracting l^r times that sum gives the integral lift. PadicCoboundaryOfDivisible proves separation: if all finite reductions of an integral cocycle are boundaries, sets of continuous finite-level primitives are finite (differences are 1-cocycles, finite by finite H1 and finite coefficients). Nested nonempty closed conditions on the compact product of coefficient values have a common primitive. Its finite reductions are continuous, hence it is continuous. Thus intersection l^r X=0; the same holds locally. Finite mod-l quotient and separation imply finite generation by successive coefficient approximation in Z_l (the remainder lies in every l^r X). No completeness of X is presupposed. This matches global_lim/local_lim.

Reduction commutes with localization on representatives. Therefore C=coker f has C/l^r C=coker f_r, whose size is the finite H0(A_r) size by PT. Likewise local Y_v/l^r Y_v has size H0(G_v,A_r) by local Tate. The dual H0 groups are precisely the l^r-torsion subgroups of the corresponding integral-discrete H0. For E nonzero they have bounded order by the earlier cyclotomic valuation bound; globally use restriction of invariants to any local place (S nonempty). Finite generation and bounded sizes imply finite C and Y, with eventual equality of cardinalities, giving the exact length equalities. This is the actual card_cok/local_tate_duality_h2_limit mechanism.

Kernel reduction needs extra care: in general ker(f_r) is not (ker f)/l^r(ker f). For r beyond the exponent of Y, l^r X lies in K=ker f, hence ker f_r=K/(K intersect l^r X). The source cok_ker explicitly conditions this comparison, and exists_kill_pi supplies the bound. On the dual side Sha1TorsionLevelFiltration defines P_r in Sha1(A) by representatives taking values in A[l^r] and local coboundary primitives in A[l^r]. The P_r increase and exhaust Sha1(A): continuous cocycles on compact G_S have finite image, and finitely many local primitives have bounded torsion exponent. The finite Sha group maps onto P_r. For r beyond a bound killing local H0(A), it is injective: if its class becomes a global boundary delta a in A, its local boundary delta b_v already has b_v killed by l^r; a-b_v is locally invariant, also killed by l^r. One place suffices, so a is killed by l^r, and the class was zero already. This is the positive-twist-range case of ptShaAt. Consequently the finite Sha perfect pairing implies |K/(K intersect l^r X)|=|P_r| for large r. Separation and the induced filtration detect length. In the present SNum context Sha is finite, so P_r eventually equals Sha; bounded increasing quotient sizes and separation force eventual zero intersection and finite K of the same size. This gives length K=length Sha1(A), without choosing compatible finite-level duality isomorphisms. This review does not audit the e=0 branch of ptShaAt, which is not needed for Lemma K.
