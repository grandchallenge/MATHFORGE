## 1. The genuine affine vanishing and its degree restriction

Let O=O_L with L totally complex, m=k+1<r2(L). The source SAff concerns homomorphisms f:H->GL_(n+b)(O) of block form [[f0(h),B(h)],[0,I_b]] with f0:H->GL_n(O). It asserts that for every real bar m-cycle, the image under f differs from the image under diag(f0,I_b) by a boundary. This is stronger than a statement about rational conjugacy or matrices with zero off-diagonal block. The case n=0 or b=0 is immediate. In general the affine group P=A semidirect Q, with A=Mat_(n,b)(O) additive and Q=GL_n(O), has section s and projection pr, and f factors into P because B(hh')=B(h)+f0(h)B(h'). It suffices to prove that pr induces an isomorphism in degree m over real coefficients with inverse s.

The source Unit lemma chooses one integral unit v whose embedding-modulus monomials of every positive degree <=m are not 1. Dirichlet's unit theorem identifies the logarithmic image of the unit group with a full lattice in the hyperplane sum log|sigma_w(v)|=0, with one embedding per complex place (the equal factor 2 is harmless). For each vector a of nonnegative integers, 0<sum a<=m<r2, at least one coordinate vanishes and at least one is positive; hence its dot product is a nonzero functional on the hyperplane. Only finitely many a occur. A full lattice cannot be covered by finitely many proper real linear hyperplanes: for a lattice basis b1,...,bd, use b1+t b2+...+t^(d-1)bd with integral t, avoiding the finitely many roots of the nonzero polynomials. In the only relevant case m>=1 the lattice dimension is >=1. This proves the source's exact Unit condition, retaining the strict degree cutoff. For m>=r2 the all-ones vector is an obstruction, not a proof that SAff itself is false in higher degrees.

A is finite free abelian. Its real cohomology is the exterior algebra of its real linear character space, by the cellular cohomology of the real torus (or the standard Koszul resolution). Over C, multiplication by v on A has eigenvalues sigma(v) for all field embeddings, repeated n*b times, via separability and L tensor_Q C = product_sigma C. The induced pullback on the qth cohomology has eigenvalues products of q such eigenvalues. For 1<=q<=m none is 1, by the Unit condition applied to the multiplicities of infinite places in the product and taking complex modulus. This is the mechanism of the actual Span/Eigen/APoly assertions. A real annihilating polynomial is obtained by multiplying the factors (X-lambda)(X-conj lambda) over the finite list of q-fold products, with f(1)!=0, including an empty list if the character space is zero. Since the source cup products span cohomology, the polynomial annihilation means the resulting cocycle is a coboundary. The standard exterior algebra argument establishes the cohomological content; it does not verify every explicit cup-span or cochain-normalization implementation in SpanCup/SpanStep.

For the conventional split-extension proof, apply the first-quadrant cohomological LHS spectral sequence of the actual extension 1->A->P->Q->1 over R. It has E2^{p,q}=H^p(Q,H^q(A,R)). The central element u=v^-1 I_n of Q acts by conjugation by s(u)^-1 on A as multiplication by v. Depending on the action convention, the coefficient action of u is this pullback or its inverse; both have no eigenvalue 1 in the degrees 1<=q<=m. Since u is central, u-1 is a Q-linear automorphism of H^q(A,R). On H^p(Q,M), the action of a central group element on M induces the identity: the natural group-homology/cohomology conjugation chain homotopy acts by the identity on the group variables and by u (or u^-1 with the opposite convention) on coefficients. Equivalently the central action on derived invariants agrees with the identity because it is identity on invariants and lifts to the derived functors. Thus u-1 induces both zero and an automorphism, forcing E2^{p,q}=0 for 1<=q<=m and every p. This does not assume semisimplicity of Q or its infinite-dimensional representations. Only the coefficient eigenvalue calculation uses diagonalization.

For total degree <=m only row q=0 remains. Incoming differentials to E_r^{p,0} have source (p-r,r-1) of total degree p-1<=m-1, and any such positive row vanishes; outgoing differentials go below row zero. Consequently inflation pr*:H^m(Q,R)->H^m(P,R) is an isomorphism (indeed the section also makes it injective). Over a field the algebraic dual of homology is cohomology, even without finite dimension, by extending linear functionals from the cycles. Thus the projection and section induce inverse homology isomorphisms, and every cycle w differs from s pr(w) by a boundary. Push through the affine block embedding to prove the exact SAff. This uses the standard LHS spectral sequence and central-action identity explicitly instead of the source's explicit Core/Transfer filtered proof. The latter is not independently replayed; its formal correspondence remains a separate obligation. No claim of proof of the entire Lean import closure.

## 2. Actual cosets and the arithmetic rank-one construction

Write Gamma_N=GL_N(O), K_N=GL_N(L), q_N=diag(pi,1,...,1), pi a prime element. Use Gq=H_N={c: q_N c q_N^-1 in Gamma_N}. Entry scaling proves exactly that c mod pi stabilizes the first coordinate line (entries below the first in column zero vanish). The determinant is unchanged by conjugation; integral matrix and integral unit determinant imply integral inverse. Thus H_N\Gamma_N is in bijection with projective lines over F=O/(pi), via c -> line(c^-1 e0). Surjectivity follows by field transvections lifting to O and determinant-one transitivity on lines; equality of lines is exactly c d^-1 in H_N. For N>=1 i_N=(|F|^N-1)/(|F|-1)>=N; N=0 is trivial and not used. The source only needs finite i_N and N<=i_N, not this sharper formula. For stabilization s(c)=diag(c,I_b) of n>=1, coset inclusion is injective because H_N intersect s(Gamma_n)=s(H_n), and its image consists exactly of lines contained in the first n coordinates (PLow). Stabilization commutes with embedding into K and with q_N. These verify pIndex,pGqStab,pStabK,pLow under the actual source conventions; no choice of canonical coset representative assumed.

For c in a complementary coset choose a lower coordinate j>=n such that v_j is nonzero mod pi for v=c^-1 e0. Choose u,t in O with u v_j+pi t=1 (field inverse mod pi). In L^N define y=(u/pi)v+t e_j and G=I+(y-e_j)e_j^T, the source rank-one matrix. Then y_j=1/pi, det G=y_j by the one-column determinant identity, and
    gamma=q_N c G = q_N c + [u e0+(t-1)q_N c e_j]e_j^T
has integral entries and det gamma=det c, a unit. Hence gamma is in Gamma_N. This is aff_gamma, including the crucial integral inverse; no assumption that u is a unit in O. For S=diag(h,I_b), since S e_j=e_j and e_j^T S=e_j^T,
    G^-1 S G = S + ((S-I)y)e_j^T.
Indeed G times the right-hand side equals S G, since e_j^T(S-I)y=0. This is aff_conj; the lower rows equal those of identity and the top-left block equals h.

For the right action of Gamma_n on H_N\Gamma_N, stabilizer H of [c] consists of h with c s(h)c^-1 in H_N. Then q_N c s(h)c^-1 q_N^-1=delta_h lies in Gamma_N, and
    G^-1 s(h) G=gamma^-1 delta_h gamma
is integral and invertible. Thus the conjugated homomorphism f of H has precisely the affine block form required by SAff. For an invariant homogeneous cocycle F on K_N, the expression F(q_N c s(h_j)) equals F(f(h_j)G^-1) by gamma-invariance. On cycles, change basepoint from G^-1 to 1 using SEv (the bar prism identity), and apply SAff to the pushforward cycle. This equals evaluation on s(h_j). It establishes pStabVan for the complementary cosets, not for all cosets. The lower nonzero residue is essential to constructing G.

## 3. Why stabilizer-level vanishing gives a global sum identity

For a real homogeneous degree m cocycle on a group H, if evaluations vanish on all cycles, its linear functional on bar m-chains factors through the boundary map into degree m-1 chains. Extend that linear functional to all degree m-1 chains. Convert it to a homogeneous invariant cochain by successive differences. The coboundary evaluates exactly as the original cocycle on all chains, so equality of cochains follows. This is PCob, with no finite-dimensional assumption.

For H a subgroup of Gamma and H-invariant Phi on Gamma^(m+1), restriction to H may have a primitive by PCob. Choose a left-coset representative r(x) of Hx, set v(x)=x r(x)^-1 in H; v(hx)=h v(x). The standard homogeneous prism between identity and v has boundary difference v_*-id (sum of m+1 signed prisms; interior faces cancel in pairs). Evaluation by the closed Phi, with sign reversed if needed, gives an H-invariant cochain primitive of Phi-v*Phi. Pull back the primitive on H and add. This proves PCobSub using the source's same equivariant retraction/prism mechanism.

For finite right Gamma-set X and family Phi_x satisfying Phi_{xg}(y)=Phi_x(g y), closed and vanishing on stabilizer cycles, choose one representative x0 per orbit and an H_x0-invariant primitive Psi_x0 on Gamma by PCobSub. For each x=x0 g_x, use Psi_x(y)=Psi_x0(g_x y). Changing g_x changes it by a left stabilizer element, so this is independent of that choice. In particular Psi_{xg}(y)=Psi_x(gy). Summing over X yields a Gamma-invariant primitive of sum_x Phi_x, because right action permutes X. Hence the sum evaluates to zero on Gamma-cycles. This reconstructs PTransfer exactly, including multiple orbits and nonnormal stabilizers; individual summands need not be Gamma-invariant or exact on Gamma.

The Hecke average T F(y)=i_N^-1 sum_[c] F(q_N c y) is independent of representatives by the defining subgroup and Gamma_N invariance. Right multiplication permutes the cosets, giving invariance; finite summation commutes with the homogeneous coboundary and with real linear combinations (PHeckeFormal). If F has zero evaluation on all Gamma_N cycles, apply the finite-action argument to the entire coset set with Phi_c(y)=F(q_N c y)/i_N. On each stabilizer conjugate h by q_N c into Gamma_N and change basepoint using SEv; its evaluation vanishes by the hypothesis. Hence TF also has zero evaluation on all cycles (PHeckeKer). This is not an assumption that TF itself is identically zero as a function.

## 4. Exact transfer identity PKey

Split H_N\Gamma_N into the i_n stabilized cosets and complement X. Both are invariant under the right action of s(Gamma_n). On X use Phi_c(y)=F(q_N c s(y))-F(s(y)), as functions on tuples in Gamma_n, with embedding into K_N understood. Their equivariance follows from coset representative independence and F-invariance. They are closed. Section 2 gives zero evaluations on each stabilizer. Section 3 gives zero evaluation of their sum on every Gamma_n cycle. Thus the contribution of X, after evaluation, equals (i_N-i_n) times the original evaluation on the stabilized cycle. On the i_n stabilized cosets, q_N=s(q_n), and the term is precisely the small Hecke average of the restricted cocycle, multiplied by i_n. Dividing by i_N gives
    ev_N(T_N F,s_*z)=(1-a) ev_N(F,s_*z)+a ev_n(T_n res F,z), a=i_n/i_N.
This is the actual source PKey, with normalization and coset direction checked. Crucially it is an equality on cycle evaluations, not an equality of the raw functions obtained by deleting the complementary correction. The source explicitly keeps a coboundary sum at the cochain level. The affine argument explains why most large cosets contribute an unchanged evaluation.

## 5. Finite evaluation space and slow-decay theorem

Fix n=2m+4 as in Assemble. The source SFin supplies a finite set S of real cycles representing a spanning set modulo boundaries. It follows from the bar bridge and finite generation of integral H_m(Gamma_n,Z), via lifting a finite set of generators of the scalar-extended homology. The source uses FinitelyGeneratedTorsion/HglFGOf and FgShift: actual exact tuple-complex resolution, finite orbit counts and finite stabilizers give finite generation by permutation-module homology and the long exact sequence. Conditional on that genuine geometric resolution and orbit/stabilizer hypotheses, finite generation follows by dimension shifting: positive-degree homology of a finite-stabilizer orbit module is finite (Shapiro and finite-group integral homology), degree-zero is free with finite rank. From 0->kernel->C0->Z->0 and its analogues, recursively descend the degree and use exactness and finite generation over Z. The geometry itself is still the shared torsion frontier; no inference of it from finite calculations. Standard arithmetic group finite-generation theorems could provide conventional alternatives, but not used to silently discharge the source's actual geometry. SStab is the earlier conditional actual stabilization isomorphism in degree m at n>=2m+4, still awaiting audit of its proof.

Let V be the real vector space of invariant closed homogeneous cochains on K_n. Define P:V->R^S by cycle evaluation and Q:V->R^S by evaluation after T_n. If P(F)=0 then F vanishes on all cycles by SFin and boundary annihilation, hence Q(F)=0 by PHeckeKer. Therefore Q factors through im P. The induced linear map on the finite-dimensional subspace im P is bounded, say ||Q(F)||_infty<=M||P(F)||_infty, M>=0 independent of F (extend to all R^S if desired). This is lift_map_comp; V itself may be infinite-dimensional. The empty set S case is immediate.

Let i_n be the fixed positive index, and take N>=N0=n+1+ceil(5(1+M)i_n). Then N>n, i_N>=N and a(1+M)<=1/5. The order of quantifiers matters: N0 depends on L,m,pi and the fixed finite evaluation system, not the later choice of F or cycles. For invariant closed F at N put x_j=(ev_N(T_N^j F,s_*z))_{z in S}, y_j=Q(res T_N^j F). PHeckeFormal preserves closedness/invariance and stabilization restriction preserves both. PKey gives x_(j+1)=(1-a)x_j+a y_j, with ||y_j||<=M||x_j||. Reverse triangle gives
    ||x_(j+1)|| >= (1-a)||x_j||-a M||x_j|| >= (4/5)||x_j||.
Hence ||x_j|| >= (4/5)^j||x_0||. The actual SHecke hypothesis provides for every cycle z a constant C_z such that |ev_N(T_N^j F,z)|<=C_z(3/5)^j, for all j. Apply to finitely many stabilized cycles, sum absolute constants to get ||x_j||<=C(3/5)^j. Combining yields ||x_0||<=C(3/4)^j for all j, hence x_0=0. By SFin all smaller cycles have vanishing stabilized evaluation. By SStab every larger cycle is homologous to a stabilized smaller cycle, so all its evaluations vanish. This proves precisely SHecke under the stated SFin/SStab and conventional affine/cohomological inputs. The lower rate is a lower bound on the finite vector norm, not necessarily any one coordinate. No uniform bound on all cycle-specific C_z required. No claimed nonzero actual class satisfying both rates.
