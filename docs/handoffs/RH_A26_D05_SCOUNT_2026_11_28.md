## Exact arithmetic quotient
Use additive Y=F*, I=Div(F), d=div, T=mu(F), K=ker d=O_F*. Set g=gamma^-1 on Y, gI divisor permutation so d g=gI d; n=ord gamma, a=(1+ell^c)^E>=2; delta=g-a, deltaI=gI-a. Both g^n=gI^n=1. deltaI injective since a^n-1 nonzero and I torsion-free. U=d^-1(deltaI I), W=U/(delta Y+T), EQ=K/(delta K+T), ClQ=I/(dY+deltaI I). Class group C=I/dY finite by classical fractional-ideal factorization and class-number finiteness (clFin). Map U->C by y->deltaI^-1 d(y) mod dY. It descends to W (delta y maps to d y, torsion maps to 0). Image exactly ker(deltaI induced on C): if deltaI i=d y then y in U and maps to [i]. Kernel consists of classes of units: if i=d z then y-delta z in K. Intersection K with delta Y+T is delta K+T by injectivity of deltaI. Thus exact 0->EQ->W->ker(deltaI:C->C)->0. Finite endomorphism has equal kernel/cokernel orders and ClQ is its cokernel. Once EQ finite, |W|=|EQ||ClQ|. EqHom is precisely Hom(ClQ,Z/ell^nu) by free divisor basis: principal divisor and twisted relation vanish iff two defining conditions. Source proves injection, converse immediate. Hence |EqHom|<=ell^v_ell(|ClQ|) by finite abelian group decomposition.

## Unit determinant (cardEQ, places, unitLat, latDet, permDet)
F/L cyclic and L totally complex. Each complex place of L has n extensions, transitively/free under Gal (fix an embedding L->C; each place above it has unique embedding extending the chosen embedding, since conjugate restriction differs). Thus r=r2(L) disjoint n-cycles. Dirichlet logarithms of units, modulo roots of unity, form full lattice in sum-zero hyperplane in R^(nr) (all weights 2, common scaling immaterial). Equivariant for inverse Galois action. Add constant-vector Z to obtain full lattice in ambient real space, with operator g-a on hyperplane and 1-a on line. This is actual source augmentation, not an assumption of integral permutation basis of units. On each cycle permutation block, determinant absolute a^n-1; full determinant (a^n-1)^r. Integral lattice index equals determinant absolute by basis and Smith normal form. Thus |EQ|*(a-1)=(a^n-1)^r, finite. LTE v_ell(a-1)=c+v, v_ell(a^n-1)=nu+v for v=v_ell(E), n=ell^(nu-c), c>=1 and c>=2 for ell=2. Proof by binomial raising to ell increases valuation by one (higher terms have strictly higher valuation under c constraints); exponent prime to ell preserves it. Hence v_ell(|EQ|)=r(nu+v)-c-v. This is exact, including n=1.

## Weighted norm map and kernel (kerF, fewLe)
N=sum_{j=0..n-1} a^j g^(n-j), acting on Y, equals source weighted multiplicative norm. Telescoping gives N delta=delta N=(1-a^n)g. Put q=ell^nu, s=ell^b, a^n-1=q u where u=ell^v m, m prime to ell. Define f:W->Y/(qY+T), [y]->[s N y]. Well-defined by identity and preservation of torsion. If f[y]=0, s N y=q z+t with t torsion. Apply delta: -q u s g y=q delta z+delta t. In Y/T torsion-free, cancel nonzero q; gives u s g y in deltaY+T, then apply inverse g (preserves denominator). Therefore u s kills kernel, i.e. ell-primary kernel exponent <=ell^(b+v). Few bounds |im f|<=B: choose one representative in U for each distinct image. Each satisfies DivDel; equality of weighted s-norms modulo pure qth powers implies equality modulo qY+T and therefore equal image. Do not assume converse; mod torsion quotient only strengthens separation. Apply Few to finite set of representatives. No dependence on nu in kernel exponent.

## Bound on number of ell-primary generators
For w in W[ell], choose y in U and z in Y with ell y=delta z+t, t torsion. In Y/(ellY+T), class of z is invariant under g because ell divides a-1. Write d y=deltaI i, then ell deltaI i=deltaI d z, hence d z=ell i by injectivity. Thus z is in SelInv: invariant classes modulo powers AND roots of unity, with divisors divisible by ell everywhere. Choices yield injection W[ell]->SelInv: equal z classes imply z-z'=ell z0+t0, hence ell(y-y'-delta z0) torsion, so y-y'-delta z0 torsion. No finite generator assumption on Y.

SelL is base-field L* modulo ellth powers with valuations divisible by ell outside primes above ell. Full-divisor-divisible subgroup modulo powers maps onto Cl(L)[ell] by dividing divisor by ell; kernel image of units, size <=ell^[L:Q] (Dirichlet rank <=d-1 and cyclic roots). Allow primes above ell via valuation residues, at most d primes, contributes <=ell^d. Cl(L)[ell] elementary abelian size ell^h<=classNumber, h<=floor(log2 classNumber). Thus |SelL|<=ell^(2d+floor(log2 h_L)).

SelInv bound: choose generator rho of finite roots of unity in F. For any invariant class, choose alpha with all divisor valuations divisible by ell, gamma(alpha)=alpha v0^ell rho^j, 0<=j<ell, absorbing ellth powers of rho. Ordinary norm to L implies Norm(v0)^ell=Norm(rho)^(-j), giving <=ell choices per j, at most ell^2 pairs (j,Norm(v0)). If two classes share pair, ratio alpha/alpha' satisfies gamma(x)=x y^ell with Norm(y)=1. Hilbert 90 gives y=z/gamma(z), so x z^ell fixed and x=x0 times ellth power for x0 in L*. Faithful action on mu_(ell^nu) from Lay and LTE (order of 1+ell^c mod ell^nu=n); inertia away from ell acts trivially on these roots by injective residue reduction, hence trivial. Therefore F/L unramified at primes outside ell, valuations of x0 descend without ramification multipliers. x0 belongs to SelL, since alpha and alpha' have ell-divisible valuations. Each pair's fiber contained in translate of image of SelL (need not be injective). Thus |SelInv|<=ell^2|SelL| and |W[ell]|<=ell^G, G=2d+floor(log2 h_L)+2. This reconstructs SelInj, SelInvLe, SelLCard, unram and h90. In particular, no assumption F-unit rank bounded in nu; descent controls only the ell-rank of W.
