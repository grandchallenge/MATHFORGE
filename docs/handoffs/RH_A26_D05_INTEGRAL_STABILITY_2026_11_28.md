## Dedekind stable range
Exact StableRangeLe R s means every unimodular row of length n+1 with n>=s shortens to unimodular row by adding multiples of last entry. Let row (a_1,...,a_n,b), n>=2, in Dedekind domain. If all a_i zero, b unit, add b to a first coordinate. Otherwise choose j with a_j nonzero, k != j. Nonzero principal ideal has finitely many maximal prime divisors. CRT choose t congruent 1 at those containing a_k and 0 at the others. Replace a_k by a_k+t*b. A maximal ideal containing entire new row must contain a_j. If it omits a_k, t=0 mod P leaves a_k nonzero. If it contains a_k, t=1 mod P and original unimodularity forces b outside P, since all other a_i lie in P. Both impossible. This proves dedekindSR without PID assumption.

## Split exactness dependency and boundary
Actual splitAcyclic_step inducts on ambient n, using commuting horizontal and vertical deletions on pairs (split list of length a, unimodular list of length b annihilated by all covectors of first list). Vertical exactness on a+b=p follows from standardizing split list, reducing to uniSeq in free complement rank n-a; b+s<=n-a. Horizontal exactness on a+b=p+1 and b>=2 uses induction on splitSeq at smaller rank and link reduction. For fixed unimodular list w length b, extend by duals gamma. In any link with prefix sigma whose covectors kill w, correct each vector by subtracting projection onto gamma; dual pairings unchanged. The corrected prefix appended to gamma gives split list rho of length b+len(sigma); the restricted link identifies with unrestricted link of rho. Standardization reduces to rank n-b-len(sigma); exactness condition 2(a-len(sigma))+s+1<=that rank follows from 2a+b+s+1<=n. For b>=2 and a+b=p+1 this follows 2p+s+1<=n. Zigzag: for simultaneous h,v-cycle c in (a,b) with a+b=p, lift vertically y, then h y is simultaneous cycle. By induction on a lift h y vertically to z' with h z'=0. Horizontal exactness yields z'=h u; y-v u is h-cycle lifting c. At b=0 get h-cycle in (p,1) lifting original c. Each singleton-w slice needs only bounding in whole split complex, not slice exactness at unavailable one-extra-rank threshold. Source moves cycles to entries also annihilated by chosen dual of w using link elimination at nonempty prefixes, then cones with (w,dual). This covers b=1 exceptional degree without stronger horizontal exactness assumption. Supporting link elimination and uniSeq exactness via coordinate/affine induction remain exact-source correspondence obligations; literature proves KStability independently.
