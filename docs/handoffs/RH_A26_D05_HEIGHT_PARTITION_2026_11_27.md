## 1. Exact height and Basic

Let O=O_L, r=number of infinite places, N>=1. On V=product_w C^N, Q_h(z)=sum_w z_w* h_w z_w, for h in the positive Hermitian cone P. The embedded lattice Lambda=O^N is discrete; for totally complex L it is full real rank d=[L:Q]N=2rN by the Minkowski embedding. For fields with real places it is full in the real/complex Minkowski subspace, still discrete in V. Minimum lambda(h)=min_{0 != v in Lambda} Q_h(v)>0, achieved because bounded ellipsoids contain finitely many lattice points; there is a nonzero v for N>=1. The source's dual h^D has blocks (h_w^-1)^T, not h_w^-1 alone. It is positive and smooth. The action ac(g)h=(g^-1)* h g^-1 gives Q_ac(g)h(v)=Q_h(g^-1 v); and dual(ac(g)h)=ac((g^-1)^T)dual(h). For integral invertible g both act by lattice bijections.

Relative closeness Cl(delta,h,k) means (1-delta)h <= k <=(1+delta)h in Loewner order; testing vectors supported at one place proves equivalence. For delta<=1/2, k positive, inverse order gives Cl(2delta,h^D,k^D). If E_w=h_w^-1/2(k_w-h_w)h_w^-1/2, its operator norm <=delta, so |u*(k-h)v|^2<=delta^2 Q_h(u)Q_h(v) at each place; in particular source Basic's 4delta^2 bound. These prove Basic with the source positive cone and relative order.

Write lm(B)=min_{v!=0} sum_w ||B_w v_w||^2. Then ht(x)=(lm(x^-1)lm(x^T))^-1. Indeed dual((x^-1)*x^-1)=(xx*)^T=(x^T)*x^T. Under left multiplication by integral gamma both factors are unchanged by lattice bijections gamma^-1 and gamma^T. For the source dist1(B)=(1+sum_{w,i,j}|B_wij-Iij|)^2, the Euclidean norm inequality gives lm(AB)<=dist1(A)lm(B). Hence
    ht(xg)<=dist1(g) dist1((g^-1)^T) ht(x).
The reverse inequality follows by using g^-1 on xg. Both factors continuous in g with value 1 at identity, proving continuity by squeeze and compact-translation bound by maximum. All minima positive; alternatively lm(identity)>=1 because a nonzero integral coordinate has norm at some place at least 1 by the integer field norm.

For the lower bound retain source I0=|disc L|^N d^d. If lambda(h)>=1, in an integral basis the real Gram determinant G satisfies 1<=(4d)^d det G. Conventional proof: open metric balls of radius 1/2 about lattice points are disjoint, so their volume <= covolume; the Euclidean ball contains the cube of side 1/sqrt(d), hence even the weaker displayed inequality holds. Apply to h/lambda(h) and h^D/lambda(h^D). Determinant products reciprocal, hence (lambda(h)lambda(h^D))^(Nr)<=I0^2. Since Nr>=1 the product <=max(1,I0^2), proving ht>=1/max(1,I0^2)>0. This establishes actual SHt with source lower-bound and distortion witnesses, using standard covolume formula and volume argument for detlow, not an original proof-term replay.

## 2. Uniform counting and smooth minimum

This is the actual Count bound (ambient dimension includes real-place complex slots).

## 4. Transference and the dual lower bound

For completeness reconstruct TransZ in conventional lattice coordinates, the covering-radius estimate used by source TransZ. For a full Euclidean lattice Lambda of rank d with squared dual minimum >=delta>0, Minkowski volume argument on lattice and dual gives product of their squared minima <=(4d)^2 (same Gram lower bound above, using reciprocal determinants). Hence a shortest vector v has squared norm <=(4d)^2/delta. It is primitive, extends to an integral basis (gcd of coordinates one), and orthogonal projection to v-perp gives a full discrete rank d-1 lattice. The projected dual is exactly Lambda^* intersect v-perp, since its pairings with projected vectors equal pairings with Lambda; its squared minimum >=delta. Inductively approximate the orthogonal component with squared error <=C_(d-1)/delta. Correct along v by rounding the remaining scalar coefficient, squared error <=||v||^2/4. The two errors are orthogonal, giving C_d=C_(d-1)+(4d)^2/4, or the source's looser recurrence using shortest bound 2(4d)^2. d=0 trivial with positive witness. This proves TransZ contract for any positive definite G by transporting metric to Euclidean space. It is the source shortest-vector induction mechanism, not proof-term replay of TZ.core.

In integral-basis coordinates let nonzero integer functional c have Gram dual norm c^T G^-1 c. By nondegenerate trace pairing, multiply its trace-dual representative by discriminant D_L to obtain f in O^N nonzero (adjugate of integral trace matrix). Then D_L c(z)=2 sum_w Re(f_w^T z_w). Dual norm of the right-hand functional equals 4 lambda_form(h^D,f), by Riesz representation z_w=h_w^-1 conjugate(f_w). Hence c^T G^-1 c>=4 lambda_D/D_L^2. Applying TransZ gives actual Trans with C1=C_d D_L^2/4 and Q_h(z-v)<=C1/lambda_D. This explains why source h^D is not literally the metric dual of O^N without the discriminant factor.

## 5. Thick-part compactness and proper action

Fix c>0 with both minima >=c, put rho=C1/c. The covering estimate gives a vector of Lambda within squared error rho of any point of V. Short lattice vectors of length squared <=10rho span L^N: otherwise a nonzero L-linear functional annihilates them, and their real embeddings lie in a proper real subspace of V. Choose a normal vector Z of squared norm >rho (e.g. 2rho); its approximation v within squared rho cannot lie in the subspace, while Q_h(v)<=2Q_h(Z)+2rho<=10rho. Contradiction. Select N L-linearly independent short vectors as columns of A. Hadamard at each place gives |det A_w|^2 det h_w <=(10rho)^N. Using detlow on h/c and product of determinants proves
    |Norm(det A)|<=I0 ((10rho)/c)^(Nr).
For integral A invertible over L, its row O-module is a finite-index sublattice of O^N of Z-index |Norm(det A)|, by determinant of the real/rational multiplication operator. There are finitely many such row lattices of bounded index (each contains J! O^N for integer J bounding the indices), and two matrices with identical row lattices have row bases related by GL_N(O). This proves the finite HNF representative consequence actually needed in GThick (not the optional source cardinal bound). Choose a finite representative set B for A under left multiplication. After action by that gamma, the quadratic forms on columns B bounded by 10rho, and the lattice minimum >=c. For fixed B, invertibility at each place and positivity bound all eigenvalues from above in fixed coordinates by Hadamard/trace in the B basis. detlow supplies a positive lower bound for product of determinants; with all upper eigenvalue bounds this gives individual positive lower eigenvalue bounds. Therefore the closed set of forms satisfying these inequalities is compact in P, including source thKB closure (minimum conditions closed; limit remains positive by determinant bound). Finite union gives Thick. This is the source short-basis/finite-HNF proof, with conventional finiteness argument instead of source HNF enumeration cardinal witness.

Proper: for compact K subset P its eigenvalues are uniformly bounded above and away from zero. If h and ac(gamma)h lie in K, uniform Loewner bounds force entries of gamma^-1 at all embeddings bounded. Minkowski lattice discreteness gives finitely many such gamma, including for real places. This proves GProper, also with K enlarged to union of two compact sets. It is not properness of the continuous group action without its compact isotropy.

## 6. Bump, normalization and partition assembly
