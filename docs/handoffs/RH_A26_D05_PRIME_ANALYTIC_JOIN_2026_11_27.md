Exclude prime ideals of norm <= B, finite by ideal norm finiteness and injectivity of height-one spectrum into ideals. Pick a remaining principal prime ideal p=(pi). Nonzero ideal implies pi != 0, and prime principal ideal implies Prime pi in the integer ring. Norm > B implies >= B; take B=25. This consumes Hilbert class field, Artin correspondence, Chebotarev and ideal finiteness, not a new proof of those imported formal developments. Applies to all number fields; no total-complexity hypothesis in SPrime.
