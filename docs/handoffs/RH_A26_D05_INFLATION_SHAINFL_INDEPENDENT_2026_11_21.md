## The exact statements, in conventional notation

Let `F` be a number field, `Ω` an algebraic closure, `G=Gal(Ω/F)` with its profinite topology, and `S` a finite set of nonarchimedean primes of `F`. Let

```text
N = closure(normal subgroup generated by all I_w at w over v outside S)
Q = G/N
q : G → Q.
```

For a Hausdorff topological `R`-module `M` with continuous `Q`-action (inflated to `G`), `InfUnram` asserts that
```text
inf : H¹_cont(Q,M) → H¹_cont(G,M)
```
is injective and its image consists **exactly** of classes whose restriction to `H¹(I_w,M)` is zero for all inertia subgroups outside `S`.

Select one place `w_v` above every `v∈S`. The original `Sha1` consists of classes in `H¹(Q,M)` whose restriction via each `D_{w_v}→Q` is zero. The original `ShaAbs` consists of classes in `H¹(G,M)` whose restriction to every decomposition subgroup above `S` is zero and to every inertia subgroup outside `S` is zero. The claimed `shaInfl` equality is

```text
inf(Sha1(Q,M,{D_(w_v)})) = ShaAbs(G,M,S).
```

This is an equality of cohomological submodules, not just a dimension count.

## Independent proof 1 — InfUnram

Take a continuous normalized 1-cocycle `f:G→M`, with
```text
f(gh) = f(g) + g·f(h).
```
Because the `G`-action factors through `Q`, every `n∈N` acts trivially on `M`. If the restriction of the class `[f]` to any inertia group `I_w≤N` is zero, then `f|I_w` is a coboundary. Since `I_w` acts trivially, that coboundary is the *zero function*: `f(i)=i·m-m=0`.

Let `K={n∈N : f(n)=0}`. Since `f|N` is a group homomorphism, `K` is a subgroup. The cocycle identity implies
```text
f(gng⁻¹) = g·f(n)              (n∈N).
```
Thus `K` is normal in `G`. Since `M` is Hausdorff and `f` is continuous, `K` is closed in `G`. It contains every outside-S inertia subgroup, hence the *closed normal subgroup they generate*, exactly `N`. Therefore `f|N=0`.

Now `f(gn)=f(g)` for all `n∈N`; it descends uniquely to a cocycle `f̄:Q→M`. Continuity descends because `q` is a topological quotient. The class `[f]` is its inflation. Conversely, an inflated cocycle vanishes on `N`, so it has zero inertia restrictions outside `S`.

Inflation is injective: if `f̄∘q` is the coboundary `g·m-m`, then surjectivity of `q` shows `f̄(γ)=γ·m-m` for every `γ∈Q`. Thus `[f̄]=0`.

**Conclusion:** the full `InfUnram` cohomological mechanism is independently proved, relying only on the explicit closed-normal-generation definition, continuous cocycle/quotient facts, and Hausdorffness. Neither a Poitou–Tate hypothesis nor the Bernoulli arithmetic estimate is used.

**Boundary checks:** (a) Without the outside-S inertia condition, `H¹(G,M)` can contain classes that do not descend; (b) without the action factoring through `Q`, an inertia coboundary need not be the zero cocycle; (c) without continuity/Hausdorffness, a zero set need not absorb a *topological closure*. These assumptions may not be silently removed.

## Independent proof 2 — ShaInfl local-condition equality

First, for any selected place `w_v`, inflation of a quotient cocycle commutes with restriction to the decomposition group:
```text
res_{D_(w_v)} ◦ inf_G = inf_{D_(w_v)} ◦ res_{q(D_(w_v))}.
```
The map `q|D_(w_v):D_(w_v)→q(D_(w_v))` is surjective, so its H¹ inflation is injective, by the same coboundary/surjection argument as above. Therefore a quotient class is zero on the original local `D_(w_v)→Q` exactly when its inflated class is zero on `D_(w_v)`.

Second, every place `w'_v` of `Ω` over the same finite prime `v` is a Galois translate of `w_v`. Consequently `D_(w'_v)` is conjugate to `D_(w_v)`. Vanishing of a cohomology class on one conjugate implies vanishing on all conjugates, via the cocycle identity and transport of coboundary witnesses. This is standard Galois place transitivity; the original source has the exact lemma `exists_smul_eq_of_isPlaceOver` in `ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/Global.lean`. One independent classical route is transitivity on primes in every finite Galois subextension together with profinite compactness of compatible automorphisms. The full foundational proof of that valuation lemma was **not** independently replayed in Lean.

Both directions now follow:
- `[c]∈Sha1` ⇒ its inflation vanishes on all decomposition groups above `S` (selected places plus conjugacy) and all inertia outside `S` (because they lie in `N`), so it lies in `ShaAbs`.
- `[f]∈ShaAbs` ⇒ `InfUnram` gives a **unique** quotient class `[c]`; its restrictions at each chosen `D_(w_v)` are zero, and local inflation is injective, so `[c]∈Sha1`.

Hence `inf(Sha1)=ShaAbs`, under the stated standard Galois valuation/continuity foundations. This **independently reconstructs the mathematical proposition expressed by `shaInfl`**; it does not establish the source Lean proof as kernel-checked.
