## General chain bound (KH.shift, main, mainFace)

Let P_p=Z[X_p], equivariant permutation modules in an augmented exact chain complex ending in a G-module M. Exactness at P_0 through P_m, augmentation surjective. At most A orbits and stabilizer orders <=s for p<=m, s>=1. Final differential columns P_(m+1)->P_m have l1 norm <=b, b>=1. Claim Tor H_m(G,M) finite of order <= b^A*s^(m*A*s^m).

Base: H_0(G,M)=coinvariants. Right exactness identifies it with Z[G\\X_0] modulo images of relation columns. Mapping to orbits sums coefficients and cannot increase l1. In free Z^a, a<=A, let L span these short columns, possibly infinitely many. Select a maximal Q-independent subset of relation columns, extend to a full Q-basis by standard unit vectors. Its integral lattice N has index |det|<=product column l1<=b^a (Leibniz expansion or Hadamard). Torsion of Z^a/L is Sat(L)/L, Sat(L)=(Q-span L) intersect Z^a. Intersection Sat(L) with N consists only of selected relation lattice L0 by independent complementary basis. Thus Sat(L)/L is a quotient of Sat(L)/L0 which embeds in Z^a/N. This is cokerTors, indexLe, torsSat and base; no finite generation of the set of relations assumed.

Positive degree j: for transitive orbit G/H, permutation module is Ind_H^G Z and Shapiro gives H_j(H,Z). For finite H, h=|H|, composite equivariant norm Z->Z[H] and augmentation is h id. Shapiro for trivial subgroup gives zero positive homology of regular representation, hence h kills H_j(H,Z). Degree-j bar chain group has h^j basis elements; subgroup of cycles free of rank <=h^j, so quotient killed by h has order <=h^(h^j). For <=A orbits with h<=s use finite direct sums (or source permSplit/LES induction) to get |H_j(G,P_p)|<=s^(A*s^j).

For 0->K->P_0->M->0, exact sequence H_m(G,P_0)->H_m(G,M)->H_(m-1)(G,K). If U->V->W exact and U finite, restricting second map to Tor V gives kernel <=|U| and image <=|Tor W|, hence finite Tor V and product bound. Shift resolution by one (imageRep); needed orbit/stabilizer bounds retained and final column bound unchanged. Induct m. B0=b^A, Bm<=s^(A*s^m)*B_(m-1); gives b^A*s^(A*sum_(j=1..m)s^j)<=b^A*s^(m*A*s^m). Source uses latter bound. For ordered tuples with face deletion each column has m+2 signed summands, repeated faces only reduce l1, so b=m+2. ## Arithmetic geometry

L totally complex, d=[L:Q], D=abs discriminant, N>=1, b0=d*N. Let h=(H_w) Hermitian per complex place, q_h(y)=sum_w y_w^* H_w y_w, y in O_L^N. Ry={q_h(y)>=1 for nonzero y}. Real lattice basis yields Gram G with real determinant 2^b0 det G=D^N (prod_w det H_w)^2: realification each complex Hermitian block has determinant square and complex embedding covolume squared D/2^d per copy. Nonnegative on integral vectors implies PSD by rational scaling/density. For positive definite G=B^t B, Minkowski box (or Blichfeldt with oversized box) gives a nonzero lattice vector in B Z^b0 of coordinate abs <=2 |det B|^(1/b0), hence 1<=(4*b0)^b0 det G. Apply to G+epsilon I and pass epsilon->0, obtaining positive determinant and positive definiteness. Real embedding isomorphism then gives positive definite H_w. I0=D^N*b0^b0: Gram inequality gives 1<= I0 prod det H_w (use b0>=2 and D>=1). Local finiteness follows from uniform positive lower eigenvalue on neighborhood and finiteness of lattice points in bounded boxes. Ry nonempty by scaling identity using discreteness of embedded lattice. PSD cone closed, positive-scaled, contains Ry, positive dual nonempty by trace.

Vertices have active linear constraints q_y(h)=1 spanning the dual. Source Sx consists of ordered tuples of vertices minimizing a common linear functional strictly positive on nonzero PSD cone. Normal cones closed convex sets covering positive dual. For positive functional coercivity on PSD unit sphere gives compact sublevels; choose extreme minimizer. Local finiteness means nonspanning active equations permit small +/- perturbation, contradicting extremality. On compact convex hull of finitely many positive functionals every minimizing vertex lies in union of their compact sublevel sets (else all functionals evaluate it strictly above fixed feasible h0 and so does convex combination). Only finitely many vertices occur there: local finiteness and compactness reduce defining active equations to finite choices. Finite supported cycles reduce to finite closed convex cover of compact convex hull. Conventional nerve proof: sufficiently small relative open convex thickenings in compact convex hull preserve all finite intersection nonemptiness patterns by compactness. Good-cover nerve theorem implies acyclicity; ordered tuple chains including repetitions have same augmented homology via acyclic carrier on simplex full tuple complexes (prepend fixed vertex contracts each). Pick N independent common minimal columns A with nonzero det. For each vertex h pullback blocks H'_w=A_w^* H_w A_w have diagonal entries <=1, since each column has total q_h=1. Hadamard yields det H'_w<=1 and >=0. Product equals I prod det H_w, I=|Norm(det A)| positive integer. Thus I<=I0 and each det H'_w>=1/I0.
