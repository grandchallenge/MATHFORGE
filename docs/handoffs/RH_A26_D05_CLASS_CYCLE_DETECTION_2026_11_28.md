## SDetect
Q=ell^nu. F contains primitive Q-root. For beta in F*, take x^Q=beta and finite splitting field K=F(x), Galois abelian over F. If nontrivial choose nonidentity sigma; Chebotarev gives positive-density primes with Frobenius sigma. Absolute residue degree >=2 primes have count O([F:Q] sqrt(X)) up to norm X, hence density zero; exclude ramification, primes over Q, denominators/numerator of beta and specified nonzero b. At degree-one prime, residue field is prime field and O_L reduction surjective. x is a local unit; all Q-th roots of unity distinct after reduction since residue characteristic prime to Q (if eta reduces to 1 and eta !=1, sum_{j=0..Q-1}eta^j=0 reduces to Q=0). Hypothesis beta reduces to Qth power in residue units, so polynomial splits there, Frobenius fixes reduction of x; sigma(x)/x is Qth root reducing to 1, hence sigma(x)=x, contradiction. Thus beta in F*^Q. Q=1 immediate. Original source instead uses dense absolute degree-one Frobenius in absolute Galois group and valuation proof; exact Chebotarev formal dependencies remain open.

## SClass inputs and filters
Notation E=e+1, M=2e+3, Q=ell^nu, w=ell^c, kappa=1+w, A=Z/Q. Lay requires cyclic Galois F/L generated by gamma, order ell^(nu-c), primitive zeta_Q with gamma zeta=zeta^kappa, 1<=c<=nu, c>=2 if ell=2. DivDel: ord_P alpha=d(gamma P)-kappa^E d(P). Weighted norm N(alpha)=prod_j gamma^j(alpha)^(kappa^(Ej)), j<ord gamma. P(X)=prod_{j=0..M-1}(X-kappa^j), P_E omits E. beta=prod_i gamma^(-i)(alpha)^[X^i]P_E. Valuation equivariance gives ord_P beta=sum_i [X^i]P d(gamma^i P) because P=(X-kappa^E)P_E. Residue log f on b-units (all finite valuations zero away from b) is additive, and b in O_L makes group Galois invariant. Let chi_cyc(sigma) satisfy sigma(zeta)=zeta^chi_cyc(sigma). Then sum_sigma chi_cyc(sigma)^E f(sigma beta)=P_E(kappa^E) f(N(alpha)): expand log, reindex sigma=tau gamma^i, use multiplicativity of chi_cyc and sum over cyclic generator powers. No division by P_E(kappa^E).

SRel: base zc in degree 2E is sum of all bars [j1|1|...|jE|1] in additive cyclic C_Q; boundary cancels paired adjacent merges by translations on the summation indices and the two end effects include multiples Q (last deletion pairs with last merge). More directly last merge/end are paired by translating last j. H_(2E)(C_Q^2,A) free A-module rank 2E+1=M: tensor the two periodic free resolutions with differentials alternately g-1 and norm; after trivial A all vanish. Therefore M+1 pushforwards by d_t:x->(x,tx) have a relation with some coefficient unit modulo Q: lift to Z^M, find rational linear dependence, clear denominators and divide common gcd in free Z^M before reduction (not in modular homology). Q=1 zero ring case trivial. This reproduces SRel via periodic resolutions rather than its source's generator induction.

SEta: J=A^2, chi_t(x,y)=x+ty. Construct eta in integral group ring Z[J] whose pushforward zero for t!=t0 and selected pushforward sum_i P_i [kappa^i]. Let T act on Z[X] by substitution X->X^kappa and I=(X^w-1). T acts as identity mod I on Z[X], and acts as kappa^j on I^j/I^(j+1), since T(X^w-1)/(X^w-1)=sum_{r=0..kappa-1}X^(wr)=kappa mod I. Thus P(T)X in I^M. Evaluate X->[1] in Z[A_add], giving selected pushforward divisible by ([w]-1)^M. For each t!=t0, t0-t divides w in A since 0<|t0-t|<=M<w and ell-adic valuation <=c; hence [t0-t]-1 divides [w]-1 by geometric series. Product D=prod([t0-t]-1) divides selected pushforward, choose Q0 with D Q0 equal to it. Multiply prod_{t!=t0}([(0,1)]-[(t,0)]) by first-coordinate embedding of [-sum_{t!=t0}t] Q0. At nonselected t a factor maps to zero; at t0 shift cancels, leaving desired value. No inversion in group ring. This is actual ExpStep.sEta.

## Lattices and prism join
Use fractional-ideal coordinate lattice Lambda_eta with valuations >=eta(j)d(P), Lambda_0=O_F^J. Choose b in O_L nonzero, multiple of splitting denominator and norms of primes in support d. Then both lattices localize to O_F[1/b]^J, and any L-linear lattice transport preserves that localized module by clearing powers of b. repD(C_Q^2) acts diagonally by roots of unity, preserving both lattices. For nonselected t group coordinates by chi_t, sums of divisor exponents zero from eta pushforward. SStein gives F-linear block maps determinant 1 transporting standard block lattice to target. SStein independently follows from Steinitz classification of sums of fractional ideals over Dedekind domain: determinant ideal ratio principal generated by beta implies isomorphic modules; extend isomorphism to F, ratio of determinant to beta unit, adjust one target coordinate by inverse ratio. Empty block requires beta=1. Actual source constructs determinant-one two-coordinate moves. For selected t, on chi_t(j)=kappa^i mod Q twist coordinate by gamma^(-i), so zeta^(kappa^i) becomes zeta. Well-defined because kappa mod Q and gamma have same order ell^(nu-c) (standard prime-power order with c>=2 for ell=2). Other labels unchanged. Twisted block at label 1 has total divisor ord beta by polynomial identity; others zero. Use SStein with determinant beta at label1 and 1 otherwise. Conjugate block maps back. Each g_t carries Lambda_0 to Lambda_eta and commutes with rho d_t, rho=repD. All g_t in localized group G. Let H stabilizer of Lambda_0 there; rho and sigma=g0^-1 rho g0 take values in H, k_t=g0^-1 g_t in H. Let B fill sum n_t (d_t)_* zc. Exact prism P(g) satisfies dP+Pd=conj(g^-1)_*-id. Thus
y=sigma_*B-rho_*B+sum n_t P(k_t)(sigma d_t)_*zc
is an H-cycle, since conj(k_t^-1) sigma d_t=rho d_t. In ambient group y homologous to sum n_t P(g_t)(rho d_t)_*zc by applying prism g0 to B and P(g0*k_t)=P(g0)+P(k_t)conj(g0^-1) on cycles modulo boundaries. This is actual CChain; no commutation between arbitrary g_t needed. Multiply by n_t0^-1 (unit), not by inverse of interpolation factor.

## Residue evaluation construction and integral realization
SCoc: k* cyclic of order Qr, choose integer-valued log lg with lg(ab)=lg(a)+lg(b) mod Qr and lg(zeta^x)=rx mod Qr (generator log multiplied by Bezout coefficient). U(a,b)=(lg(a)+lg(b)-lg(ab))/(Qr) integer symmetric 2-cocycle, w(a)=v(a) in additive A a 1-cocycle. Cup U^E cup w mod Q is degree 2E+1 cocycle; insertion prism on commuting cyclic group evaluates as U^E times w(g), adjacent insertions cancel by symmetry leaving final insertion positive. On zc under chi_x, sum_j U(zeta^(xj),zeta^x)=lg(zeta^x)/r by telescoping (integer), congruent x mod Q, hence prism value x^E w(g). Exact zc, differential and prism conventions match HasVal/FProd. STrans transfers this cocycle along projective lines: choose one nonzero representative per line, homogeneous scale function lambda, right action a(g) line=g^-1 line, scalar cocycle c(g,line)=lambda(v)/lambda(g^-1 v). Bar transfer sums consecutive cocycle values along prefix action, is chain map by multiplication law and permutation reindexing first face. Change of section gives coboundary twist, realized by conjugation in semidirect product (k*)^X semidirect G and prism chain homotopy. On direct sum V+W projective lines map equivariantly to P(V) or P(W) according to first component nonzero; fibers have sizes |k|^dim W or 1, hence both 1 in A since Q divides |k|-1. Section can be chosen from nonzero first component else second, giving additive transferred evaluation on cycles. Dimension-one recovers original cocycle. STheta: prism multiplicativity makes value additive in g for scalar cyclic action; homomorphism to A is conjugation invariant and block additive. Transvections die by conjugating with diagonal scalar a!=0,1, since commutator expresses all transvections; a exists for Q>1 via zeta. Diagonal/transvection generation implies factorization through det, and one-dimensional value gives HasVal. Q=1 trivial. SPhi follows by reduction commuting with block stabilization/replication; trivial representation evaluated zero in positive degree. These are conventional reconstructions of exact source mechanism, not independent kernel validation of all supporting identities.

CSplit: choose integral trace-dual families by clearing denominators, b0 in O_L nonzero, b0*x=sum_i Tr(x f_i)e_i. Galois independence gives sum_i tau(f_i)sigma(e_i)=b0 delta_(tau,sigma). For reduction r with r|O_L onto k and b0 nonzero mod r, this identity constructs arbitrary residue vectors (r(sigma x))_sigma; trace identity proves its kernel is ker(r|O_L) O_F by expressing b0*x as linear combo with coefficients in base kernel and inverting b0 modulo kernel. Thus O_F tensor k is product k^Gal, also after inverting b with r(b)!=0. Localized lattice stabilizer G acts on k^(Gal x J) by reduction; integrality condition of H lets it act on M=O_F^J. This is finite projective over O_L (finite torsion-free over Dedekind), choose free module M+P=O_L^N and extend action by identity on P. After reduction, exact same representation plus trivial complement by tensor direct sum. This resolves potential nonfree ring-of-integers obstruction, not a claim that O_F has free integral basis over O_L. CFree/CInt/CTriv are actual interfaces. Under conjugate coordinate twists, reduction splits into blocks (sigma,label x), scalar cyclic eigenvalue zeta^(chi_cyc(sigma)*x) and determinant reduction of sigma(delta_x). By IsAdd/HasVal, evaluation of each prism equals sum_{sigma,x}(chi_cyc(sigma)*x)^E f(sigma delta_x), where delta_x all 1 for nonselected t; selected delta_1=beta, others 1. This is CVal/CGtEq/CJoin. Since f(1)=0 and n_t0 invertible, normalized H-cycle mapped to GL_N has evaluation P_E(kappa^E) f(N(alpha)), exactly SClass. Rank N and b chosen before r, v, theta, important for joint injection. All bad primes in finite support excluded uniformly for each alpha. ## SFew and precise valuation loss
Let finite S consist of elements alpha satisfying DivDel and pairwise separated by N(alpha)^(ell^B) modulo F*^Q, B=2e+2+M(c+M)=2E+M(c+M). Choose SClass witnesses for each alpha, exclude primes dividing ell times product b_alpha. Eligible primes P also require base reduction onto k_P. For each choose v_P:k_P*->C_Q with kernel Qth powers (cyclic group with Q dividing order via faithful reduction of roots, except Q=1 trivial), and additive theta_P with HasVal; primitive Phi_P from SPhi. Consider the ONE product-valued primitive family Phi=(Phi_P)_P with B-target product A^I, potentially infinite I. Define f(alpha)=ell^(2E) Phi_(N_alpha)(y_alpha). If f(alpha)=f(alpha'), all residue logs of ratios of weighted norms killed by ell^(2E) p where p=P_E(kappa^E). In integers, for distinct i,j<M, v_ell(kappa^i-kappa^j)<c+M: write d=|i-j|, kappa^d-1=ell^c(d+ell^c t); if ell^M divides parenthesis then ell^min(c,M) divides d, impossible since 0<d<M<ell^c and M<ell^M. Thus v_ell(p)<M(c+M) for M>=3. In A p divides ell^(M(c+M)) by unit inversion of prime-to-ell part (if valuations >=nu, target also zero). Do not cancel p or ell^(2E). Equality implies ell^B times residue log of ratio vanishes, equivalent to residue of (N(alpha)/N(alpha'))^(ell^B) a Qth power. SDetect yields global Qth power, hence alpha=alpha' by Few separation. Thus f injective. Apply SUpper directly to finite image in product A^I (no finite index assumption), k=2E, N*=4E+6, giving |S|<=Q^r2(L) tc(L,4E+6,2E+1) tc(L,4E+6,2E). STors at E then supplies log torsion budget <=2*(2D)^((2Ed)^24). This is exact SFew contract under Lay, M<ell^c and M<r2(L); not a bound on arbitrary classes or EqHom yet.
