# HC-001 current-status and known-boundary audit

**Audit date:** 2026-07-24  
**Campaign:** `HC-001`  
**Parent:** `grandchallenge/MATH-PROGRAMME#65`

## Determination

The classical rational Hodge conjecture remains open. The official Clay status page continues to list it as unsolved and identifies complex dimension four as the first unknown dimension.

The exact target is cycle-class surjectivity

```math
CH^p(X)\otimes Q -> H^{2p}(X,Q)\cap H^{p,p}(X)
```

for every smooth projective `X/C` and every `p`.

## Elementary known boundary

Let `n=dim_C X`.

- `p=0` and `p=n` are immediate from connected components and points/fundamental classes, with the usual componentwise convention.
- `p=1` is the Lefschetz `(1,1)` theorem.
- `p=n-1` follows from the divisor case and hard Lefschetz: the inverse of multiplication by a hyperplane class preserves rational Hodge structures, and multiplying an algebraic divisor class by the required hyperplane power produces an algebraic codimension-`n-1` class.

Consequently, the full conjecture holds for all smooth projective varieties of complex dimension at most three. The first unrestricted new case is

```text
n=4, p=2.
```

This conclusion uses only the boundary cases above; it does not catalogue all special classes known in higher dimension.

## Selected higher-dimensional known case

Zucker proved the Hodge conjecture for smooth cubic fourfolds. This is a genuine codimension-two fourfold result, but it is restricted to a special family and cannot be promoted to arbitrary fourfolds.

## False neighboring formulations

### Integral coefficients

The naive integral Hodge conjecture is false. Atiyah and Hirzebruch used cohomology operations to exhibit projective examples with integral Hodge classes not represented by integral algebraic cycles. The rational coefficient ring in the classical conjecture is therefore structural, not cosmetic.

The audit does not infer that every refined integral cycle statement is false. Torsion-free, degree-restricted, or variety-restricted integral questions require separate records.

### Compact Kahler replacement

Projectivity cannot be dropped. The official problem account records a compact-Kahler counterexample to the analytic-cycle analogue, and Voisin constructed compact Kahler examples whose rational Hodge classes are not generated by Chern classes of analytic coherent sheaves.

These are boundaries on generalization, not counterexamples to the projective rational conjecture.

## Distinct theorems and conjectures

- Algebraicity of Hodge loci is a theorem of Cattani-Deligne-Kaplan. It says the parameter locus where a fixed class remains Hodge is algebraic; it does not construct an algebraic cycle representing that class.
- The generalized Hodge conjecture concerns coniveau/support of Hodge substructures. It is not merely another wording of cycle-class surjectivity.
- The variational Hodge conjecture concerns transport of algebraicity in families. A proof cannot assume that transport as a formal consequence of remaining type `(p,p)`.
- Absolute Hodge and motivated classes are powerful substitutes and stability notions. Neither label is itself an algebraic-cycle certificate.
- The Tate conjecture is an arithmetic parallel. Reduction modulo a prime requires explicit specialization, comparison, and lifting bridges before it bears on the complex statement.
- Algebraicity of inverse Lefschetz operators or Kunneth projectors is a standard-conjecture-type obligation. These correspondences cannot be assumed in a proof whose purpose is to establish their algebraicity as a consequence of Hodge.

## Current computational status

No numerical computation decides the universal conjecture. Period matrices and lattice-relation searches can identify candidate rational Hodge classes in explicit families, and symbolic intersection calculations can test proposed cycles, but exact equality in rational cohomology and exhaustive generation remain theorem obligations.
