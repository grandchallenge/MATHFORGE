## What this source governs

The organizer event page identifies the PDF above as the **Competition and Judging Handbook** and states that scoring, judging, eligibility, and the submission cutoff are governed there.

The same organizer page also states that a score-bearing claim must:
- be encoded as an exact formal artifact or approved formal certificate;
- reproduce in a pinned environment;
- disclose dependencies and material tools;
- pass machine checking;
- pass human semantic-fidelity review.

It further states that:
- human teams retain authorship;
- the official workflow uses Hill and Climb submission with immutable artifact/evaluator/provenance linkage;
- organizer-curated focus problems receive a 1.1x focus bonus;
- other open problems require a precise canonical statement, provenance, open-status review, family identity, and difficulty assessment;
- substantive new variations may qualify, while cosmetic restatements or artificial fragmentation do not.

## GCL effect

This handbook is an external **rules authority**, not a substitute for any per-hill source lock.

It may govern:
- submission eligibility;
- scoring and focus bonus;
- formal-artifact requirements;
- checker and semantic-review expectations;
- provenance and tool disclosure;
- authorship and classification requirements;
- submission timing.

It does **not** by itself establish:
- the exact identity or statement of OM26-H2 through OM26-H7;
- the evaluator/checker contract of an unresolved hill;
- the mathematical truth of a claim;
- novelty or priority;
- competition acceptance;
- MATHCERT certification.
